# ATTENTION POLICY — Technology Should Give Time Back

## North Star

The fleet exists to reduce cognitive load and screen dependence. Success is not engagement, session length, notification volume, or how often the owner checks a dashboard. Success is verified work completed while the owner remains free to live, think, move, create, and maintain real relationships.

## Interface laws

1. **Voice first when practical.** High-level intent should be speakable from a walk, car-safe hands-free context, phone call, earbud, or compatible smart-glasses interface.
2. **Screen optional, not mandatory.** Dashboards are for inspection, comparison, editing, and approvals—not for constant supervision.
3. **No infinite feeds.** Every view has a stopping cue and a finite purpose.
4. **No engagement bait.** Do not add streak anxiety, unread-count pressure, random rewards, or notifications merely to bring the owner back.
5. **Batch nonurgent information.** Default to concise scheduled briefings instead of continuous pings.
6. **Interrupt only for consequence.** Time-critical, L3/L4 approval, credible failure, or explicit VIP events can interrupt.
7. **One next action.** When human input is needed, ask for the smallest decision that unblocks the mission.
8. **Background work is normal.** Long-running agents keep working without requiring the owner to keep a window or terminal open.
9. **Fun must point outward.** Character animation, music, humor, achievements, and 3D scenes may celebrate real verified outcomes; they should not become a reason to compulsively check the system.

## Modes

### Walking mode

Jarvis captures short voice intent and context. Responses are optimized for audio: outcome, proof, decision needed, one next action. Detailed artifacts remain available for later review.

### Focus mode

The fleet continues L0-L2 work silently. Nonurgent updates queue. Only qualifying interruptions pass through.

### Digital Sabbath / off-screen block

During a user-defined off-screen period, the fleet may continue safe internal work and monitoring, but nonurgent notifications and approvals queue until the period ends. No risky action is upgraded merely because the owner is unavailable.

### Review mode

A finite dashboard or 3D observation session shows current missions, evidence, decisions, failures, and next gates. The user should be able to leave after the review without losing state.

## Briefing format

Voice/glasses default:

`WHAT HAPPENED -> PROOF -> WHAT NEEDS YOU -> ONE NEXT ACTION`

If nothing needs the owner, end with a clear stopping cue such as: `No decision is needed. The system is continuing in the background.`

## Attention budget

Each mission records estimated and actual human attention cost. The proactive loop should prefer two equally valuable actions when one requires less human switching, checking, or supervision.

Repeated unnecessary interruptions are a Lightning defect signal and should produce an improvement proposal.

## Phone role

The phone is a transport and sensor, not the destination. Use it for voice, camera, authentication, urgent approvals, location/device capabilities, and short confirmations. Move deep work, background execution, indexing, generation, and monitoring off the phone whenever practical.

This policy adapts the healthy-technology principle of keeping technology that is genuinely useful or enjoyable while adding boundaries against compulsive, default checking. It is not an abstinence policy.
